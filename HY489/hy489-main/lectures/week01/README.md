## The data

`../../labs/lab01/data/` — the same synthetic cohort the whole course uses: 1,174 patients,
their encounters, conditions, observations and medications, in the Synthea export
schema. Generated by a seeded script, so every student gets identical numbers.

Nothing in it describes a real person. It keeps the identifier columns (`SSN`,
`DRIVERS`, `PASSPORT`, `LAT`, `LON`) on purpose: they are a teaching point here and
the whole exercise in Lab 5.

## What this segment is for

The five questions escalate deliberately. One to three are pandas warm-ups. Four
needs a filter and a sort across two tables. Five has no code answer:

> Find one thing that looks too clean to be real data.

That question is the point of the segment. The cleanliness that makes synthetic data
safe to hand out is exactly what hides the problems the rest of the course exists to
solve, and students who notice it themselves remember it.
